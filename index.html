The club

Padel, performance
& community.

The Padel Project is Musaffah's flagship padel destination — six premium indoor superpanoramic courts, a rooftop lounge with a games area, a relax patio and prayer room, plus Among the Stars serving food and drinks from our in-house coffee shop.

Since Padel Beast took over day-to-day management, occupancy and player engagement have grown exponentially — this is where the community started, and where it keeps growing, one match at a time.

Facilities
